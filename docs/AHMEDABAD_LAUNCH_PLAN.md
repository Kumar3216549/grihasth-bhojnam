## 1. Delivery clusters / डिलीवरी क्लस्टर

Deliver in **clusters**, not all over the city. One rider can cover one cluster per lunch shift.

| Cluster | Areas (already in the website) | Status at launch |
|---|---|---|
| **East (start here) / पूर्व** | Odhav GIDC → Kathwada GIDC → Naroda GIDC | ✅ Active |
| **South / दक्षिण** | Vatva GIDC → Narol → Aslali | Vatva & Narol active on the site, take orders only when you have 2 riders |
| **West / पश्चिम** (Sarkhej–Bavla highway) | Changodar → Moraiya → Sanand GIDC | Off — phase 3 (after 150 tiffins/day) |

**Why start East?** Odhav, Kathwada and Naroda sit close together, have many small and mid-size units (plastics, engineering, chemicals, textiles), and a large Hindi-speaking migrant workforce — exactly the ₹80 thali customer. A kitchen in or near **Odhav / Nikol / Bapunagar** can reach all three within one lunch window.

**Kitchen location tips:** ground-floor shed or shop with water + drainage, within 15–20 minutes of your first 10 customers, parking for the scooter, and inside AMC limits (simpler licences).

## 2. Day 1–15 survey — Ahmedabad version

- Pick **50 units across Odhav, Kathwada and Naroda** (about 20 + 15 + 15). Google Maps → search "GIDC Odhav industries", "Naroda GIDC pvt ltd", etc.
- Visit at **shift change (about 8 AM / 8 PM)** and **lunch (12–2 PM)**. Confirm each factory's real lunch time in the survey — then update the shift list in **Admin → Settings** (default: 12:30 PM, 1:30 PM, 7:30 PM, 11:30 PM).
- Talk to workers in **Hindi**, to owners / HR / accountants in **Gujarati or Hindi**. Carry the Gujarati rate card below for owners.
- Add one extra question to the Google Form: **"खाना कैसा पसंद है? — Gujarati style (sweet dal) / North Indian style / Dono"**. Default thali is North-Indian style (no sugar in dal) because most workers are from UP, Bihar, MP and Rajasthan; offer Gujarati dal on request for local staff.

## 3. Free Sample Day — where

Shortlist 5 units with **100+ workers** from the survey (bigger plants are usually in Naroda and Vatva). Ask the owner/HR in Gujarati:

> *"સાહેબ, અમે ઘરે બનાવેલું ટિફિન ફેક્ટરીના ગેટ પર ₹80 માં આપીએ છીએ. તમારા એક વિભાગના 30 કામદારોને એક દિવસ મફત જમાડવાની પરવાનગી આપશો?"*
> (Sir, we deliver home-made tiffin at the factory gate for ₹80. Will you allow us to feed 30 workers of one department free for one day?)

## 5. Ahmedabad calendar — plan around these / कैलेंडर

| When | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Uttarayan (14–15 Jan)** | Many units shut 1–2 days | Pause monthly plans in admin, add the days back with **+ days** |
| **Monsoon (Jul–Sep)** | Waterlogging on some GIDC roads (Vatva, Narol, Odhav underpasses) | Leave 20 minutes earlier; raincoat + sealed tiffin bag for rider; tell customers on WhatsApp |
| **Navratri (Sep/Oct)** | Late nights, many workers fasting | Offer a simple *farali* option on request; night-shift tiffin demand rises |
| **Diwali (Oct/Nov)** | Many GIDC units close 5–10 days; migrant workers travel home | Pause all plans for the shutdown, extend end dates; run "Welcome back" offer after |
| **Summer (Apr–Jun, 42 °C+)** | Food spoils faster | Cook closer to delivery, insulated boxes, add buttermilk (chaas) as a ₹10 add-on idea |

## 6. Licences in Ahmedabad (verify with a local CA)

- **FSSAI Basic Registration** on the FoSCoS portal (state: Gujarat). From 1 April 2026 the basic-registration limit is ₹1.5 crore turnover.
- **Shop & Establishment (Gumasta) registration** with AMC if the kitchen is inside Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation limits; outside AMC (e.g. Changodar, Sanand) check with the local body.
- **Gujarat Professional Tax** registration once you hire staff.
- **GST** only after ₹20 lakh annual turnover (or if a big company insists on a GST invoice).
- **Udyam** registration (free) — helps with bank loans and bulk company vendor forms.

*General information, not legal or tax advice.*
